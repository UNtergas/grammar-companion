# Fixtures

Test corpus for the checker. Built in M1, reused as the benchmark in every later milestone.

## Must cover

- English and French
- Sentences **with** known issues, and clean sentences where no issue is expected
  (the second group measures false positives)
- Accented characters, emoji, French guillemets `« »`, and the no-break space before `; : ! ?`
- Your real mistakes and your real vocabulary (names, jargon, franglais)

## Format

Your M1 decision. It must let a test state *which* span is wrong and *what* is expected.

## Privacy

Don't commit real emails or messages. Rewrite or synthesize them first.
